## Predictor head: inherited setting, not an experimentally justified choice

The user asked why the head uses 512 GELU units. The implemented structure is:

```text
Frozen encoder -> raw first-token (CLS) embedding of dimension D
               -> Linear(D, 512) -> GELU -> Linear(512, 2)
```

Only the head trains, using cross-entropy on two logits. There is no head dropout or normalization layer. This is a nonlinear probe, not a linear probe.

The 512-unit width was retained from the existing project default to establish the repaired baseline; it was not selected by validation or shown to be optimal. GELU enables nonlinear combinations of embedding features, but its advantage here was not tested. Do not invent a stronger retrospective justification for either choice.

With 233 training examples, head capacity is a plausible concern. NTv2's decreasing training loss and increasing validation loss are consistent with overfitting; they do not establish that the 512-unit width caused it. A regularized linear head is an important proposed control. Current scores reflect the encoder, CLS representation and this particular head together.
